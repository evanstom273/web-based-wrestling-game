## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

An experimental, serious browser-based 3D professional wrestling game exploring human characters generated entirely from code. The creator lets players shape and dress a wrestler while seeing the result immediately.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The fixed stack and ownership boundaries are in AGENTS.md and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md. Characters use procedural geometry, not imported character assets. Creator work stays separate from match simulation and controls. Desktop, landscape phones, tablets and unfolded foldables are first-class; narrow portrait phones retain the orientation gate. Changes ship on feature branches through unmerged pull requests for user review.

## Current Creator Brief

Repair shoulder straps on male and female tops, increase anatomical fidelity, expose muscle mass from smooth to ripped, and make customization feel like a game creator rather than a debugging tool. Keep inspection tools available in an advanced section. Local saving is a creator convenience, not integration into match selection.

## Evidence on Hand

Actual WebGL captures are in docs/model-lab. User-provided screenshots identify broken shoulder straps; these must be checked from elevated views as well as the normal studio cameras.
